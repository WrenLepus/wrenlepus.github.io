My Music

Introduction

Hello! This is one of the places where I share my music & compositions! I started learning music at seven and was almost immediately enthralled by what it had to offer.

After completing RCM Level 8 in piano and music theory, I continued to self-learn piano and picked up composition. Over the years, I am lucky to have over a thousand people follow my journey on YouTube.

Compositions

I view music as an approximation of the language of the soul. For this reason, the music I compose must remain sincere and faithful to my inner voice. Each piece becomes an attempt to translate personal emotions and reflections into sound.

Recent work

This piece was written last year (I take painfully long to learn a piece) and is essentially a fantasia/poem meant to mark the end of a familiar period of my life and the beginning of a new one.

The opening section is dreamlike (sognando), as if the older memories are not your own but someone else's, somehow implanted into you. I don't know if that makes any sense.

In the middle section, there are different motifs interweaving with one another. See if you can hear or find some of them, some appear inverted, either upside down or reversed left to right. You may also notice that some pages are very cramped. That's because I was short on manuscript paper at the time, so my apologies if you're a score reader and have to strain your eyes a bit.

The third section is melodically the same as the first. This is also the first time I've used three staves in piano music, so that was pretty exciting for me.
